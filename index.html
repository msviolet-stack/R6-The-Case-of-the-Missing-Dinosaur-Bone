CASE BRIEFING

The Visitor’s Missing Bone

A famous palaeontologist visited the fictional FuChong Primary School to teach pupils about dinosaur bones. He brought a dinosaur bone and left it on the classroom display table while the pupils went for recess.

At 10.43 a.m., the teacher returned and discovered that the bone was missing. Detective Maisarah was called to investigate.

Your task: Identify who took the bone, what they used to carry it, and where the theft happened.

Investigation rules

Exactly one of the four suspects took the bone, acting alone. It was taken between 10.39 a.m. and 10.43 a.m. and was not returned during this period. Each suspect carried one different item: a school bag, trash bag, metal water bottle or lunchbox. All recorded evidence is accurate.

Suspect statements are claims to check against evidence. The CCTV records and teacher’s confirmed observation are facts.

Timeline & Evidence

Clue 1 · 10.31 a.m. · Canteen

CCTV showed Katie Low eating with friends, with her lunchbox beside her.

Clue 2 · 10.33 a.m. · Library

CCTV showed Sarah Winchester entering the library with her school bag.

Clue 3 · 10.36–10.37 a.m. · Classroom

Zane Tan entered the classroom and left one minute later carrying his metal water bottle.

Clue 4 · 10.38 a.m. · General office

CCTV showed Jayden Teo entering the general office. At 10.38 a.m., the classroom camera was switched off for four minutes. The control panel was in the general office, but the recording does not show who operated it.

Clue 5 · 10.39 a.m. · Bone confirmed present

The teacher checked the classroom and confirmed that the dinosaur bone was still on the display table. She then left. Nobody was inside when she left.

Clue 6 · The only entrance

The classroom had one doorway and no other usable entrance or exit. Continuous corridor footage covered that doorway throughout 10.39–10.43 a.m.

Clue 7 · 10.40 a.m. · One visitor

Corridor CCTV showed Jayden entering the classroom at 10.40 a.m. He was the only person to enter between the teacher leaving at 10.39 a.m. and returning at 10.43 a.m.

Clue 8 · 10.42 a.m. · A suspicious bag

Corridor CCTV showed Jayden leaving the classroom with a trash bag containing a rigid shape. The contents could not be identified from the footage.

Clue 9 · 10.43 a.m. · Bone missing

The teacher returned and discovered the bone missing. The thief had carried it out inside the item they were recorded carrying.

Clue 10 · What the evidence does not prove

There is no evidence that Jayden faked being unwell. His visit to the general office does not, on its own, prove that he switched off the classroom camera.

Teacher Case Solution

Jayden Teo · Trash bag · Classroom

The bone was still present after Zane’s visit. The classroom was empty when the teacher left at 10.39 a.m. Jayden was the only person to enter before the teacher returned at 10.43 a.m. and discovered it missing. With exactly one suspect acting alone, Jayden is the only possible thief.

The thief carried the bone out inside their recorded item. Jayden left with the trash bag, so this was the container used.

The camera outage is suspicious context, but does not establish who operated the controls. The evidence does not prove that Jayden faked illness.
